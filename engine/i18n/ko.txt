# value the app puts in; a counting string has a form for each plural of the
# language, "one: … | other: …", and {n} is the count. tTerm, tTerm Web and
# names of terminals and machines are not translated. The page of tTerm Web
# has its own words, in tterm-web: these are the app's alone.
